# Competence and communication

**Sample / Portfolio Assessment** — fictional FinFlow Technologies.

Role requirements name the required skills, assessment method, accountable development
owner and review date. An evaluation retains that exact requirement revision, a
minimized subject reference, source evidence and demonstrated outcome. Training
attendance never automatically produces a COMPETENT result. Development-required
results need an action and deadline; a subsequent evaluation retains the previous one.
The sample includes one draft requirement and no invented employee assessments.

Personnel evaluations are restricted to maintainers even for GET requests. Use opaque
subject references and keep source personnel evidence in G06's MAINTAINERS scope.
The public audit trail records that an evaluation occurred, not its personnel details.

Communication plans record topic, audience, method, owner and timing. Delivery needs
actual dated evidence and an outcome. Policy-awareness plans reference a published
document revision. Individual awareness acknowledgements reuse G05's existing
revision-specific document acknowledgement lifecycle; a briefing does not forge them.
The application records delivery, but does not send messages outside the platform.

Routes: `/people/requirements`, `/{ref}/evaluations`, `/people/communications`
and `/people/communications/{ref}/deliver`.
