# Applicable obligations

**Sample / Portfolio Assessment** — fictional FinFlow Technologies.

An obligation records its source and version, jurisdiction or contractual scope,
accountable owner, review date and linked control. A contractual counterparty may
link to the supplier register. Applicability starts unassessed. Approval requires
current Head of Legal & Compliance business authority, an authored rationale and
evidence. No seed user receives that authority automatically.

Evaluation is separate from applicability. An action-required evaluation links an
existing open remediation with an owner and deadline. Every decision retains the
source revision, actor and evidence. Editing an obligation clears its current
approval, while retaining earlier decisions. The API does not infer legal duties,
produce a GDPR compliance percentage or claim a SOC 2 attestation.

Routes are `/obligations`, `/{ref}` and `/{ref}/decisions`. The sample supplier
contract entry remains an author task until the actual agreement is supplied.
